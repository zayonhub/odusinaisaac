About the designer

Thoughtful design.
Practical experience.

Based in Nigeria. Working with businesses, organisations and creative teams.

I’m Odusina Isaac, a graphic and web designer with more than seven years of experience. My practice spans brand identity, packaging, promotional communication and WordPress websites.

Through independent projects and Zayon Creative Studios, I work across digital and physical formats. My production experience informs how I think about layout, materials, legibility and handoff.

I also contribute design support to public-health initiatives, including Foot Impact.
