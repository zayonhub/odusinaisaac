About

Designer / Art Director
Isaac, at work

I'm Isaac, a brand designer working across social media design, brand identity, and product packaging. I like design that has a job to do: a post that stops a thumb, a logo that reads at a glance, a box that sells itself on a crowded shelf.

Every project starts the same way, understanding what the brand needs to say and to whom, then finding the palette, type, and structure that says it without shouting.
