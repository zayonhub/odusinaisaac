Wingate Exotic Hotel

Room offers translated into clear, image-led hotel promotions.

My contribution
Graphic design · Promotional artwork
Discipline
Hospitality campaigns

The brief & scope

The selected work promotes room offers for Wingate Exotic Hotel. Each piece needs to communicate the accommodation, incentive and booking information in a single composition.

Lead with the offer

The January artwork makes the 20% discount the largest element beneath the room photograph. Location, offer dates and contact details sit in a separate information area.

Adapt the layout to the format

The July email artwork uses a tall layout, stacked room photographs and a prominent 10% offer. The format gives the hotel experience more space while keeping the incentive easy to find.

What the work shows

The finished promotional layouts demonstrate clear offer hierarchy and adaptation across compact and vertical formats.
