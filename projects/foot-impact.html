My contribution
Brand identity · Graphic design
Discipline
Public-health identity

The brief & scope

Foot Impact is a public-health organisation. This selected identity study focuses on the mark and its presentation, rather than claiming a complete campaign system from a single image.

Connect the symbol to the name

The footprint gives the identity an immediate visual connection to Foot Impact. The curved lines beneath it create a distinctive supporting shape.

Show contrast and scale

The presentation places white and black versions side by side, with smaller and larger lockups. This makes the reversed treatment and relative readability easy to compare.

What the work shows

The selected identity board brings the positive and reversed treatments together, making the mark easy to assess across light and dark backgrounds.
