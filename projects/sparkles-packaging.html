The brief & scope

This project is presented as a focused logo study. The available artwork shows the symbol itself, so the scope stays with the identity rather than an unshown collection of applications.

Give the symbol movement

The red and blue forms create a flowing central shape, balanced by smaller spark-like elements.

Keep the presentation direct

A plain background lets the silhouette and colour relationships carry the piece without decorative mockups.
