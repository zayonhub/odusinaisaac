Yellow Yolk Feeds

Feed-sack artwork balancing brand recognition and product information.

My contribution
Packaging design · Visual direction
Discipline
Feed packaging

The brief & scope

The packaging presents Yellow Yolk Feeds Layer Mash on a sack format. The front panel brings together the brand, product name, supplier information and a storage instruction.

Build a readable front panel

The brand sits above the product name, while smaller operational details are grouped below. This separates recognition from the information a buyer reads more closely.

Compare visual treatments

The two presentations retain the central brand and product hierarchy while changing the illustration and surrounding treatment. They are shown as design variants, not as different products.

What the work shows

Two packaging mockups show the artwork in context. These images demonstrate the design presentation; they are not photographs of a verified production run.
